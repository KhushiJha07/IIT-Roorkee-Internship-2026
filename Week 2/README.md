# Week 2: Advanced CNN Architectures & Vision Transformers

## Overview
This week focused on the evolution of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), analyzing highly utilized architectures to understand their structural improvements and historical significance in computer vision. Additionally, I explored the inherent drawbacks of CNNs and how Vision Transformers (ViTs) address these limitations.

## Key Topics Explored
*   **LeNet & AlexNet:** Understanding the foundational architectures and the shift towards deeper networks.
*   **VGGNet:** The impact of using smaller, uniform filters (3x3) to increase depth and capture finer features.
*   **ResNet (Residual Networks):** Solving the vanishing gradient problem using skip connections/residual blocks.
*   **DenseNet:** Enhancing feature reuse and reducing the number of parameters through dense connectivity.

## The Shift to Attention Mechanisms
*   **Drawbacks of CNNs:** Analyzed limitations such as a lack of global context understanding and translational invariance challenges.
*   **Vision Transformers (ViTs):** Explored how treating image patches as sequences and utilizing self-attention mechanisms improves upon CNN architectures for large-scale image recognition.
